# 🛍️ Product Research Dashboard: Consumer Insights & Feature Mapping

## 💡 The Business Problem  
E-commerce platforms host thousands of products across categories, but brands often struggle to identify what features drive engagement and how customer sentiment varies across platforms. This project bridges that gap by analyzing product performance, feature trends, and review sentiment to uncover what makes a product stand out.

---

## 🚀 The Solution  
This end-to-end analytics project transforms simulated product data into a structured Excel file and a dynamic Power BI dashboard. It uses Python to generate clean, categorized datasets and Power BI to visualize pricing, ratings, platform distribution, and customer feedback—all without relying on external libraries.

---

## 📊 Key Insights at a Glance  
**Category Trends**: Electronics and skincare dominate in volume, but home essentials show higher average ratings.  
**Platform Distribution**: Amazon leads in product diversity, while Nykaa and Flipkart show stronger engagement in niche categories.  
**Feature Mapping**: Products with eco-friendly packaging, waterproofing, and dermatologist approval show higher sentiment scores.  
**Customer Sentiment**: Common complaints include battery life and packaging issues, while suggestions focus on faster charging and fragrance-free options.

## 📷 Visual Highlights  
**Platform Distribution Pie Chart**: Shows where products are listed and how volume varies across Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, and Myntra.  
This chart validates platform-specific engagement strategies.

**Price vs. Rating Scatter Plot**: Reveals pricing sweet spots and outliers based on customer feedback.  
This visual links product value to perceived quality.

**Average Rating by Brand Column Chart**: Highlights which brands consistently deliver high-rated products.  
This chart helps identify top performers and market leaders.

**Feature Matrix Table**: Combines product specs and USPs for easy comparison.  
This table supports strategic product positioning and differentiation.

**Power BI Dashboard Overview**: Combines KPIs, filters, and visuals to create an interactive experience.  
This dashboard empowers stakeholders to explore product data dynamically.
